This plugin bundles material from multiple upstream sources:

- `agents/`, `references/`, and `scripts/` originate from `yandex-direct-skill-master`,
  which includes an MIT license in the upstream source tree.
- `docs/` originates from `yandex-direct-docs-main` and contains Yandex Direct
  documentation content for local reference purposes. The upstream skill notes
  that the content belongs to Yandex.

Use and redistribution of the bundled documentation should follow the terms of
the original content owners.
